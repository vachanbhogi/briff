# Product

## Register

product

## Users
First-aid learners, medical students, and emergency response trainees practicing basic clinical diagnostics (such as learning how to locate and verify a carotid pulse).

## Product Purpose
A hyper-minimalist, real-time training companion that utilizes WebAssembly computer vision (Pose and Hands) to guide and verify correct first-aid actions. 
- *Current Focus*: Guiding and verifying correct finger placement on the carotid pulse points, improving self-diagnostic training accuracy.
- *Future Scope*: Expanding multi-model tracking systems to train and verify other vital first-aid actions, such as **CPR chest compressions** (measuring depth and rate of hand overlaps) and the **Heimlich maneuver** (detecting proper hand wrapping and thrust positions on the upper abdomen).

## Brand Personality
Precise, raw, technical. Extremely functional, high-fidelity, and styled with a clean monospace cyberpunk aesthetic.

## Anti-references
- Overly polished, slow, bloated corporate healthcare apps.
- SaaS dashboards with redundant metric cards and colorful charts.
- Overstimulating gamified visual alerts.

## Design Principles
- **Anatomical Precision First**: Direct alignment with real carotid landmarks (directly under ears, adjacent to laryngeal cartilage).
- **Uncluttered Functionalism**: Sparse, monochrome layout (black-and-white) that prioritizes the stream above all else. No redundant graphs or buttons.
- **Zero-Latency Physical Feedback**: Instant, unambiguous neon-green canvas shockwaves that visualize beat alignment on positive target contact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast neon-on-black coordinate indicators for high visibility.
- Monospace font scale ensuring legible text.
- Horizontal camera mirroring by default to prevent spatial-movement confusion.
